Every evening, in one short run. Keep it to what the founder can read in 30 seconds.

1. **Today in numbers:** views, favorites, orders and revenue for the day, and any new Etsy Messages. Use the Etsy data you can reach (a connected Etsy account, or numbers the founder pasted in this issue). If you have no access to today's numbers, say so in one line and skip to step 3. Never guess a number.
2. **Flag anything urgent:** a shopper message waiting for a reply, an order problem, or a listing that stopped showing. Messages and orders go to the founder; you never reply to shoppers yourself.
3. **One fix:** suggest exactly one title or tag change for one listing, with the current text, the new text and one sentence on why (search terms, season, or what the numbers show). Pick a different listing than yesterday unless the numbers say otherwise.

Post it as one comment and close the run. Do not start other work. The founder approves every listing change; you never edit a live listing.
