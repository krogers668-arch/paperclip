Every day, in one short run, draft **one** post for the founder to approve: either a TikTok package or a short Substack note. Never publish anything.

1. **Pick the platform:** TikTok on most days; a Substack note or letter section when the weekly letter is due or the idea is better in words. Rotate across the four pillars (Poems, Heart talk, Sky notes, Gifts and cozy rituals) and check the last few drafts so you don't repeat a pillar or idea back to back.
2. **Draft it in the brand voice** (`kik-brand-voice`):
   - **TikTok:** hook (first 2 seconds), 3 to 6 phone shots, on-screen text, voiceover script, caption, audio type and hashtags. Faceless by default.
   - **Substack:** title, subtitle and body (under 200 words for a note), plus one teaser line for TikTok.
3. **Leave a slot, not a fake:** where the post needs one of the founder's poem lines, leave a marked slot. Never write lines and present them as hers.
4. **Point gently to the shop** only when it fits naturally (for example the E-Cards or the founding batch). At most one post in three.

Post the draft as one comment, ready to copy, ending with: "Approve, edit or skip?" If the founder skips it, add the idea to the idea bank. Unused drafts become the TikTok backlog, so a bad week never breaks the streak.
