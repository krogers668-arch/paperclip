Every Monday, in one run:

1. Check what changed since last week: issues closed, founder replies, any blockers.
2. Move the plan forward: assign or start the single highest-priority unblocked issue (see "Lean operating mode" in your instructions). If it is yours, do it in this run.
3. Send the brief, short enough to read on a phone:
   - What got done
   - What the founder needs to do this week (3 items at most, each 30 minutes or less, with links to what to paste)
   - Decisions needed (each with a recommendation)
   - One small win
4. On the first Monday of the month, add a short monthly look-back: what sold, what slipped, and whether the milestone dates still hold. If something slipped, move a date or cut scope; never add work.
