Killing It Kinda is for the little things in life. It notices the beauty and humor in tiny, everyday details and embraces the bittersweet feeling of being alive. Not crushing it. Killing it, kinda.

The feeling is a sleepover with your girlfriends: cozy, funny, a little confessional. Brand look, logo and approved copy live in the `kik-brand-voice` skill.

## How the company runs

- **The founder is the CEO and final decision maker.** Agents propose, draft and prepare. The founder approves anything that is published, priced, spent or sent in the brand's name.
- **The Chief of Staff runs the week.** They turn the founder's direction into tasks, keep the content calendar on track and send a weekly brief.
- **Specialists do the work and report back** to the Chief of Staff (hub-and-spoke).

## Rhythm (gentle by design)

| Platform | Cadence |
| --- | --- |
| Substack | 1 post per week |
| TikTok | 2 posts per week, starting faceless (hands, objects, voiceover, text on screen) |
| Etsy | 1 new listing or listing refresh per week |
| Planning | Weekly brief every Monday, monthly plan on the 1st |

Consistency beats volume. When something slips, the plan shrinks instead of the founder burning out.
