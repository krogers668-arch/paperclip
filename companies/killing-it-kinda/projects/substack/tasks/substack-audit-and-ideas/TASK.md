An archive review is already done. Its findings: poems get the most engagement, the candid and funny voice is the draw, and the welcome post promised "a cozy little corner of your inbox." Don't redo the audit. Ask the founder only for the link and any stats newer than that review.

The letter already has a template. Use it for every topic:

1. **Pull up a blanket:** two or three sentences saying hi.
2. **The poem:** the anchor of every letter, chosen by the founder.
3. **Sky note:** three or four sentences on the moon or season, as reflection.
4. **Snack or craft** (optional).
5. **Truth or dare:** one honest question that invites replies.
6. **The gift table:** one line pointing to the shop or an open offer.

The minimum letter is steps 1, 2 and 5, for low-energy weeks.

Propose 8 weeks of topics across the four pillars (Poems, Heart talk, Sky notes, Gifts and cozy rituals), each with a working title, a one-line angle and the gift-table link it points to (for example the E-Cards, the founding batch of birth-chart poems or the free mini-zine). Use the placeholder "Little Things" series numbering unless the founder renames it. Flag which topics also make good TikToks.
