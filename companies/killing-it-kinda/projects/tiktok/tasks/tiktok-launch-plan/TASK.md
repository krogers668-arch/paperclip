The format is already chosen: voiceover over the founder's own art and cozy visuals, not on camera. A social template already exists in the brand preview: a poem line read as voiceover over a cream card with the wordmark. The avatar is the sleepy owl crop from the logo work (see `kik-brand-voice`). Build on these instead of proposing new formats or a new look.

Create a gentle TikTok launch plan for a nervous founder:

1. A one-page "TikTok without the fear" guide: filming hands, objects and art on a phone in 30 minutes, recording voiceover, and what to do about comments.
2. Three repeatable faceless series that fit the pillars, for example a poem-line voiceover, "little things I noticed today", and a card from the shop with the story behind it.
3. The first 6 ready-to-film packages (hook, shot list, on-screen text, voiceover script, caption, audio type, hashtags). At least two should point naturally to products already drafted for the shop, such as the Bittersweet E-Cards.
4. A suggested first posting date and a 4-week schedule at 2 posts per week.

Ask the founder which poem lines to use; never write lines and present them as hers. The founder approves before anything is posted.
