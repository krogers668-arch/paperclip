Create a gentle TikTok launch plan for a nervous founder:

1. A one-page "TikTok without the fear" guide: faceless formats, how to film on a phone in 30 minutes, what to do about comments.
2. Three repeatable faceless series ideas (for example "little things I noticed today").
3. The first 6 ready-to-film packages (hook, shot list, on-screen text, caption, audio type, hashtags).
4. A suggested first posting date and a 4-week schedule at 2 posts per week.

The founder approves before anything is posted.
