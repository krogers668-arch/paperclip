# Killing It Kinda Visual Identity

One look, every product: soft color, one serif and a lot of air.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cream | `#FCF7EE` | Page background |
| Navy ink | `#1F2D4A` | Main text, occasional dark tile |
| French blue | `#3F6DA0` | The italic "kinda" in the wordmark |
| Blush | `#F2C0C5` | Accent, the owl's body |
| Powder | `#9BC0DE` | Accent, the stamp behind the owl |
| Gold | `#D1A346` | Rules, stamp trim, the period after "kinda." (tiny doses only) |
| Light blush | `#F9E2E3` | Panels and card fills |
| Light powder | `#E1ECF7` | Panels and card fills |
| Deep blue | `#345D8E` | Labels, secondary text, italics |
| Pale blue | `#C4D5E6` | Light borders |

- Blush and powder blue carry the soft, sleepover feeling.
- Gold only in tiny doses, like a clasp on something carefully chosen.
- Flat color only. No gradients or heavy textures.

## Type (Google Fonts)

- **Lora** (serif) for headings and the wordmark. Regular 400, wordmark 500. Use *italic* for the bittersweet parts.
- **Poppins** Light 300 for body text, shop details, captions and small print. Lots of air.
- Labels: small capitals with wide letter-spacing.

## Logo

- **Wordmark:** "Killing it" in navy Lora, then *kinda* in French blue italic, then a gold period. The gold period is the signature.
- **Primary mark (chosen): Owl on a Stamp.** A sleepy blush owl with blue cheeks, closed curved eyes, a gold beak and a gold star, on a powder-blue postage stamp with a gold double frame, a perforated edge and *kinda.* in italic at the bottom.
  - Avatar: the owl in a circle with a gold border (profile pictures, Etsy shop icon).
  - Horizontal lockup: the stamp beside the wordmark (banners, headers).
- **Monogram:** an italic "k." in a circle, on blush, powder or navy. For small spaces such as favicons and Substack headers.
- Never recolor, stretch or redraw the logo. Use the founder's approved files.

## Card layout (bittersweet cards)

A white postage stamp with scalloped edges on cream. Inside, a gold double-line frame on a blush or powder fill. Top to bottom:

1. "FOREVER KINDA" in spaced small capitals
2. A small gold dot
3. The occasion in italic ("for a hard day")
4. The message, centered in Lora, last line in italic
5. A short gold rule
6. "Killing it kinda" at the foot

**Poem cards** are different: rounded corners, a thin gold border, a spaced small-capital occasion label and an italic poem line signed by the founder. The back is cream with blue lines and "with love."

## Social and listing images

- Cream backgrounds, lots of empty space, one idea per image.
- Show real products on the stamp motif; keep text in Lora and Poppins only.
- TikTok text on screen: Lora for the hook, Poppins for supporting lines, navy on cream or cream on navy for contrast.
