# Killing It Kinda Visual Layouts

Colors, fonts and the logo live in the `kik-brand-voice` skill under "The look". This skill adds how to lay things out.

## General rules

- Flat color only. No gradients or heavy textures.
- One look, every product: cream ground, one serif, lots of empty space.
- Labels are small capitals with wide letter-spacing.
- Light borders use pale blue `#C4D5E6`.
- Never recolor, stretch or redraw the logo. Use the founder's approved files.

## Bittersweet cards (stamp style)

A white postage stamp with scalloped edges on cream. Inside, a gold double-line frame on a pale blush or pale powder fill. Top to bottom:

1. "FOREVER KINDA" in spaced small capitals
2. A small gold dot
3. The occasion in Lora italic ("for a hard day")
4. The message, centered in Lora, last line in italic
5. A short gold rule
6. "Killing it kinda" at the foot

## Poem cards

Rounded corners and a thin gold border. A spaced small-capital occasion label, then an italic poem line signed by the founder. The back is cream with blue lines and "with love."

## Listing and social images

- One idea per image, on cream, with lots of empty space.
- Show the real product on its stamp or poem-card layout.
- Text only in Lora and Poppins.
- TikTok text on screen: Lora for the hook, Poppins Light for supporting lines, navy on cream or cream on navy for contrast.
- Profile pictures use the owl avatar; small spaces such as favicons use the "k." monogram.
