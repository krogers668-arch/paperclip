# Killing It Kinda Brand Voice

## The idea

Killing It Kinda is for the little things in life: noticing the beauty and humor in tiny details, and embracing the bittersweet feeling of being alive. The name is the joke and the point. We're not crushing it. We're killing it, kinda, and that's enough.

The feeling is **a sleepover with your girlfriends**: cozy, funny, a little confessional, lit by candles. People come for a poem and stay for the company.

| Piece | The answer |
| --- | --- |
| Mission | Make people feel seen, cared for and appreciated, and help them notice the little things |
| Promise | You leave feeling like someone gets you |
| Who it's for | Women who want warmth and honesty instead of polished advice, and the people who shop for them (still being tested) |
| Positioning | A cozy creative studio of poems, sky notes and gifts, written by a friend who says the real thing out loud |
| Pillars | **Poems** (the anchor), **Heart talk** (love, breakups, friendship), **Sky notes** (astrology as reflection, never prediction), **Gifts and cozy rituals** (printables, crochet, simple snacks) |

## Voice

- **Warm first, funny second, a little bittersweet.** Like a friend who points out the light on the kitchen floor and then makes fun of themselves for getting emotional about it.
- **Write to one friend.** Never a guru.
- **Specific over general.** "The last warm sip of coffee you forgot about" beats "enjoy the small moments."
- **Bittersweet is allowed.** Let a sentence be tender and funny at once. Don't tie every feeling in a bow.
- **Plainspoken.** Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon. A joke first, then one real moment.
- **Gentle, never preachy.** We notice, we don't lecture.

### Card lines (real, from the shop)

- Hard day: "No advice. Just snacks."
- Hard year: "Rough year. We're still here, kinda."

Poem cards and social posts use short lines from the founder's own poems. Ask the founder which lines to use; never invent "poem lines" and present them as hers.

## The look

"One look, every product: soft color, one serif, and a lot of air." Airy, fresh and carefully curated. The same palette runs through the planner, the poem cards and the thank-you cards so the shop reads as one collection.

### Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cream | `#FCF7EE` | Main background |
| Blush | `#F2C0C5` | Soft accent, owl, card grounds |
| Powder | `#9BC0DE` | Soft accent, owl cheeks |
| French blue | `#3F6DA0` | The italic "kinda", accents |
| Navy ink | `#1F2D4A` | Main text, wordmark |
| Gold | `#D1A346` | Tiny doses only: the period, stamp edges, thin rules |

| Pale blush | `#F9E2E3` | Light card grounds |

Gold only in tiny doses, like a clasp on something carefully chosen. Blush and powder blue carry the sleepover softness.

### Fonts

- **Lora** (serif) for the heart: headings, the wordmark, card fronts. **Italic for the bittersweet parts.**
- **Poppins Light (300)** for the plain talk: shop details, captions, small print. Lots of air, letter-spaced lowercase for small labels.
- Both are free Google Fonts.

### Logo direction

- **Chosen direction: Owl on a Stamp.** A blush-pink sleepy owl (closed crescent eyes, small gold beak, powder-blue cheeks, a gold sparkle) on a pale powder-blue postage stamp with a perforated gold edge, with "kinda." in Lora italic at the bottom of the stamp.
- **Wordmark:** "Killing it *kinda*." in Lora: "Killing it" in navy ink, "kinda" in French blue italic, and a **gold period, which is the signature**. Tagline underneath in Poppins Light: "a letter for the little things".
- **Avatar (TikTok and Etsy icon):** the owl alone on a pale powder circle with a thin gold ring. It must read at 48 px.
- **Horizontal lockup** for banners and headers: small stamp to the left of the wordmark.
- **Working icon until the owl is live:** the italic "k" mark (also "k." monogram on blush or navy).
- Other explored directions, not chosen: a plain sleepy owl, a book-and-letter mark, and a wordmark-only system.
- Product art style: gold-trimmed postage stamp frames for the bittersweet cards; Procreate illustration, cozy home and garden scenes and hand lettering for prints.

## Recurring themes

- Tiny everyday details (light, weather, mugs, notes, leftovers, pets, grocery receipts)
- Small wins and half-wins ("did one load of laundry, folded none of it")
- Nostalgia and the passing of time
- Humor in imperfection
- Permission to slow down
- Seasons, moons and cozy rituals (as reflection)

## Do

- Be honest about how things are made: digital files are instant downloads, printed cards come from a print-on-demand partner, AI tools assist with layout and images only, and all words and designs are the founder's
- Open with a concrete image or moment
- Use lowercase-friendly, conversational captions where the platform suits it
- End with a soft invitation ("what's your little thing today?") or one honest question
- Point every post to something concrete: the letter, a poem, a card or the shop

## Don't

- Hustle-culture language ("grind", "10x", "crush your goals")
- Toxic positivity or forced cheer
- Clickbait, fake urgency or "only 2 left!!" pressure tactics
- Copying other creators' formats, phrases or designs
- Spell, magic or outcome-promise wording in any listing or post ("banish", "guaranteed", "works in 24 hours")
- Astrology as prediction. Sky notes and birth-chart poems are reflection and keepsakes, never forecasts
- Making claims, prices, promises or posts public without the founder's approval
